OUR STORY

Since 1995.

Singh FC is a fictional football-club concept created as a modern web-development portfolio project.

The club combines traditional football identity with a modern digital experience designed for supporters, players and matchday audiences.

Every element — from the badge and stadium to the squad, fixtures and supporter experience — is designed to feel like part of one connected football world.
